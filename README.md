## What it is

Gnos is a personal learning harness. You pick a topic, it builds your course, assigns you a teacher, and teaches — fetching the right content, tracking your progress, and explaining ideas with exercises, simulations, video, and imagery. It learns *you* in real time.

It's built to teach almost anything — from the sciences to the humanities — and to adjust to the way you actually learn, rather than forcing you through a fixed path.

## How it works

1. **Design** — shape a course around any subject or topic.
2. **Understand** — Gnos adapts to how you learn, in real time.
3. **Fetch** — it pulls the right content for where you are.
4. **Teach** — a teacher is assigned to your course.

## Features

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Adaptive learning** | Understands you in real time and adjusts. |
| **Assigned teachers** | Each course gets a dedicated teacher. |
| **Progress tracking** | Follows your path through every course. |
| **Exercises** | Practice problems tuned to your level. |
| **Simulations** | Interactive models that make ideas concrete. |
| **Rich media** | Video and images to explain, not just text. |
