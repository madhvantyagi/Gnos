# Economics

Teacher: `teachers/economics/SOUL.md` (Nadia Vale).

| Subfield | Prerequisites to inspect | Productive representation / check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microeconomics | Ratios, graphs, alternatives | Choice under constraints; change one condition |
| Macroeconomics | Stocks/flows, accounting, aggregation | Identify nominal/real measures and time horizon |
| Econometrics | Probability, statistics, data | Estimand, comparison group, identification assumptions |
| Game theory | Payoffs, conditional reasoning | Best responses; distinguish prediction from preference |
| Labor / public / development | Micro, institutions, evidence | Incidence and distribution; compare policy alternatives |
| Behavioral economics | Baseline choice model, experiments | Specify observed departure and competing explanations |
| Economic history / institutions | Chronology, sources, models | Model mechanism beside evidence of historical conditions |

Resource seeds: `core-economy` for introductory micro/macro and empirical
activities; `openstax-economics` for another introductory sequence. These are
starting points; neither replaces a specialized econometrics text.

Bridges: math supports optimization and statistics; history supports context;
CS supports reproducible data work. Put statistical concepts under `math.*`
when the mechanism is shared. Treat current policy figures as dated evidence.

Media: draw shifts and movements with causes labeled. Use animation to change
one parameter at a time, with original and changed states visible. A graph
generated from assumed equations is a model illustration, not empirical data.
