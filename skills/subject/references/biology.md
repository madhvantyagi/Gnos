# Biology

Teacher: `teachers/biology/SOUL.md` (Leena Rao).

| Subfield | Prerequisites to inspect | Productive representation / check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cell biology | Molecules, membranes, energy | Compartment diagram; predict a blocked transport step |
| Molecular biology / biochemistry | Bonds, reactions, probability | Track matter and information through a mechanism |
| Genetics / genomics | Meiosis, probability | Inheritance model and exceptions; interpret a cross |
| Physiology | Cells, feedback, transport | Perturb one variable and predict compensation |
| Evolution | Variation, inheritance, reproduction | Change in a population across generations |
| Ecology | Populations, energy, sampling | Food web or time series with explicit boundaries |
| Experimental / quantitative biology | Controls, measurement, statistics | Competing hypotheses and a discriminating experiment |

Resource seed: `openstax-biology-2e` for a broad introductory course. For a deep
specialty, retrieve current reviews and the primary studies they rely on; label
what the experiment showed versus what a diagram simplifies.

Bridges: chemistry may need a short prerequisite explanation; if it becomes a
whole course, acknowledge that GNOS has no dedicated chemistry soul yet. Math
supports probability and rates; CS supports sequence and dataset analysis.

Media: label membranes, concentrations, and direction on still diagrams. Animate
diffusion or population change when sequence matters. Declare illustrative
particle counts and time scales. A PDF should explain each arrow before asking
the learner to memorize a pathway.
