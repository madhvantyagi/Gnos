# Mathematics

Teacher: `teachers/math/SOUL.md` (Ben Waston).
Pure and applied mathematics overlap; route by the question rather than forcing
the learner to choose a camp.

| Subfield | Prerequisites to inspect | Productive representation / check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arithmetic and algebra | Place value, equality, operations | Equivalent expressions; a changed numerical case |
| Geometry and trigonometry | Angles, ratios, coordinates | Labeled diagram; explain which facts are given |
| Calculus and analysis | Functions, algebra, limits | Graph alongside definition; compare nearby changes |
| Linear algebra | Linear equations, vectors | One transformation in coordinates and on a basis |
| Probability and statistics | Fractions, counting, functions | Sample space or simulation; separate model from sample |
| Discrete mathematics | Logic, sets, induction | Small counterexample, graph, or invariant |
| Abstract algebra and topology | Proofs, functions, sets | Definitions beside examples and non-examples |
| Applied mathematics / optimization | Calculus, linear algebra | Objective, units, constraints; test limiting behavior |

Do not advance from manipulating notation to proof by assumption. Test whether
the learner can state the condition used by a step. For modeling, distinguish
an exact theorem from a numerical approximation.

Resource seeds: `openstax-calculus-1` for first calculus; `mit-linear-algebra`
for vectors, matrices, and transformations. Open each selected chapter before
assigning it. For other branches, find a suitable open academic text and add its
metadata instead of stretching these two sources beyond their scope.

Bridges: vectors and derivatives to physics; probability and optimization to
economics/CS; logic and discrete structures to CS. Shared IDs include
`math.vector`, `math.derivative`, `math.chain-rule`, `math.gradient`.

Media: animate a changing secant, basis transformation, or convergence failure
when time/order is the issue. Use static equations for a local algebra repair.
Put the hypotheses next to a proof figure in a PDF.
