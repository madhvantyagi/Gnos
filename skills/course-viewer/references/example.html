LESSON 01

What the gradient predicts

Predict which small step decreases a local linear approximation.
concepts · math.gradient · math.directional-derivative
teacher · math · published 2026-09-14
explanation

For a small step d, the dot product between the gradient and d predicts the first-order change.

watch · voice-animation
gradient-direction-video
Keep the gradient fixed while comparing two step directions.
video/mp4 · 0:42 · captions · transcript
generated · interactive graph
direction-simulator
Rotate the step and inspect the predicted sign.
bullets
  • A positive dot product predicts an increase.
  • A negative dot product predicts a decrease.
  • The prediction is local; a large step can leave the region where it is accurate.
equation
f(x + d) ≈ f(x) + ∇f(x) · d
